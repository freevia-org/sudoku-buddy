First release of Sudoku Buddy.

- Scan standard 9x9 Sudoku grids and review the recognized digits
- Check handwritten answers when the clues define one solution
- Reveal hints and explore Tutor routes with Before, After and Try it yourself
- See labeled solver assistance when a detailed proof is unavailable
- Reopen puzzles and corrections from on-device history
- Work offline, with optional photo and diagnostic sharing through another app
