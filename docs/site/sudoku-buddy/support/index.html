Sudoku Buddy support

Android is available on Google Play. The iPhone and iPad version is in preparation and is not yet available on the App Store.

For private help, email info@freevia.org. Public GitHub issues and attachments can be seen by anyone; do not include private photographs or personal information.

Android

Use the usage guide for scanning, correcting readings, checking answers, hints and saved history. Keep the whole grid visible, use even light, and check the recognized clues and handwriting before trusting a result.

iPhone and iPad preview

The iOS release is in preparation. These instructions describe the current port; check the website for public availability.

Open the scanner and allow camera access to scan a standard 9×9 puzzle. Keep the complete grid visible, use even light and hold still. Manual capture is available; automatic capture can be changed in Settings. Choose a photo imports a saved picture through the system photo picker without granting access to your entire photo library. If camera access was denied, enable it in the device's Settings for Sudoku Buddy, or continue with photo import.

Review uncertain cells against the photograph. Tap a square to change its digit or printed/handwritten interpretation. Read shows the recognized content; Check assesses entered answers. Hint has four stages—Where, Square, Trace and Answer—with earlier reasoning available to revisit. Solve displays a solution without replacing your saved progress. The Tutor and Strategies explain techniques and offer practice.

History keeps puzzle photos, corrections and progress. The current puzzle and study/hint position survive restarting. Delete a local puzzle from History to remove it from the device. iOS backups may include app data according to your device's backup settings; Sudoku Buddy has no account-based sync service.

Submitting a reading is optional. Review the consent form before submitting a puzzle photo and recognition evidence. Automatic sharing is off by default; if enabled, future uncertain readings and corrections may be submitted. Turn it off in Settings. Open Settings > Submission receipts to retry an unconfirmed report or delete a submitted revision. Each revision has its own receipt. Deleting a local puzzle does not delete a previously uploaded report.

Report a bug or idea lets you review the disclosure before opening an external GitHub draft. Review and edit that draft before posting. GitHub issues are public; use info@freevia.org for private support. Share sheets also let you choose a recipient for a photo or diagnostics; include only information you intend to share.

If a recognition result looks wrong, correct its clues and handwriting before trusting Check, Hint or Solve. If reporting a problem, include the app version, device/OS, steps, and whether the reading was corrected. Do not post private photographs publicly.

Reporting a problem

Include the app version, iOS/iPadOS or Android version, device model, steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened. Tell us whether you checked and corrected the recognized reading. Use email for private examples. Report submission is optional; no support account inside Sudoku Buddy is required.

Delete your data

Delete saved puzzles and refused scans from History. To delete a previously submitted report, use Settings > Submission receipts and delete each revision. Deleting a local puzzle does not delete an uploaded report. See the privacy policy.
