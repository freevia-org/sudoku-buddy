Sudoku Buddy by Freevia

The joy is in
figuring it out.

Keep your pencil. Keep your puzzle. Get a little help when you need it.

Scan a standard 9×9 Sudoku on paper—including the answers you’ve already written. Check the reading, see where you may have gone wrong, or ask for a hint that explains the next move.

Sudoku Buddy is publicly available on Google Play in supported regions. Scanning, checking and hints work offline. An iPhone and iPad version is in preparation and is not yet available on the App Store. The source is public on GitHub.

Inside the app

Your puzzle. A clearer next step.

Real Android captures from the app's Google Play screenshot set.

Scanning, checking and hints work on your phone, even offline. Handwriting, erasures and lighting can cause misreads, so review the grid first.
