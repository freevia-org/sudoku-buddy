Keep solving on paper, with a little help from Sudoku Buddy.

Scan a Sudoku from a printed page or choose a photo. Sudoku Buddy reads printed clues, handwritten answers and pencil marks, and lets you correct the reading before continuing.

CHECK YOUR PROGRESS
See which entered answers need attention without revealing the whole solution.

LEARN ONE STEP AT A TIME
Explore four hint stages: Where, Square, Trace and Answer. Follow the reasoning, revisit earlier stages, or explore solving techniques in the tutor.

RETURN TO YOUR PUZZLE
Saved puzzle photos, corrections and progress remain on your device. Reopen a puzzle from History and continue where you left off.

WORK OFFLINE
Recognition, checking, solving and tutoring run on your device. No account, ads or routine usage tracking.

SHARING IS OPTIONAL
You can submit a puzzle reading to help improve recognition, with explicit consent. Optional automatic submission is off by default. Submitted revisions have receipts and can be deleted from Settings. Public GitHub feedback opens as a draft for you to review.

Photo recognition can make mistakes. Review uncertain cells and correct the reading when needed. Sudoku Buddy supports standard 9-by-9 Sudoku.
